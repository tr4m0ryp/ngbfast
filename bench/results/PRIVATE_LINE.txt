On a proprietary binary forecasting task, a tuned NGBoost model (a seed ensemble of 8 models, three-parameter distribution) had every base learner constant because its tuned leaf size exceeded half the minibatch. Folding reduced single-row prediction for the ensemble from 1,338 ms to 29 ms p50 (46x; 5th-percentile per-call ratio 24.6x; 180 interleaved calls on a loaded 8-core host) with bit-identical outputs on every held-out row. On a tuned configuration whose trees split on two of three parameters (one third of learners constant), the gain was 1.37x, also bit-identical. This is one deployment, not a benchmark; the per-call times were not retained, so no paired SE beyond the per-call quantile is available.
